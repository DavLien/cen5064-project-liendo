# ADR-004: Soft-Delete Game Archiving and Multi-Review Event History in SQLite

**Date:** 2026-11-25 | **Status:** Accepted

## Context

Users frequently remove games from their active library or replay titles they have already completed or abandoned. Hard-deleting a game row in SQLite would permanently destroy historical play sessions and reviews, while enforcing a `UNIQUE(game_id)` constraint on reviews prevented users from logging multiple completion or abandonment milestones over time.

## Decision

In `CatalogService`, removing a game with existing progress will soft-archive it by prefixing its status with `Archived_` rather than executing a SQL `DELETE`, automatically restoring its prior status and session history if re-added later. In `SqliteReviewRepository`, we will migrate review persistence to `game_reviews_v2` without a `UNIQUE(game_id)` constraint so multiple timestamped completion and abandonment entries coexist chronologically alongside play sessions.

## Consequences

Users never lose historical playtime or past reviews when archiving or resuming a game, and the Progress timeline accurately reflects multiple playthroughs. However, queries populating the active sidebar must explicitly filter out `Archived_` statuses, and priority status reversion upon deleting a review requires checking whether the deleted entry was the most recent milestone.
